Privacy policy

This app is a private rclone setup used by one person, the owner of the Google account it connects to.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, which covers only the files this app created. It cannot see other files in the account.

What happens to the data

Backup files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload and stored in the owner's own Google Drive. Access tokens stay on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any other server, and nothing is shared, sold or used for advertising or AI training.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services. Files can be deleted in Google Drive.
